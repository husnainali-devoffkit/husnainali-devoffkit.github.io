OffKit All Tools Privacy Policy

Effective date: 10 November 2026 · App: OffKit All Tools for Android and iOS · Developer: HUSNAIN ALI

OffKit All Tools is a collection of developer and text tools (JSON, Base64, SQL, SVG, Excel, Parquet and more) that run on your phone. This policy explains what data the app handles. OffKit All Tools is published by HUSNAIN ALI. Questions: husnain12.ai42@gmail.com.

1. What you type stays on your phone

Everything you type, paste, convert, or open from a file in OffKit All Tools is processed on your device only. It is never uploaded to us or to anyone else, and the tools work without an internet connection. We do not have servers that receive your content.

2. Data stored on your device

  • Your settings: theme, app language, favourite tools.
  • If you allow it in Settings, the names of tools you used recently (never their input or output).

This data stays on your device. You can clear recent tools in Settings, and uninstalling the app removes all of it.

3. Clipboard and files

OffKit All Tools reads the clipboard only when you tap Paste and writes to it only when you tap Copy. Files are opened only when you choose them in the system file picker, and saved only where you choose.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

OffKit All Tools is free and shows banner ads provided by Google AdMob. To show ads, limit how often you see them, measure their performance and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share with Google:

DataPurpose
Device identifiers (Android advertising ID, or the iOS IDFA if you allow tracking; app set ID)Advertising, analytics, fraud prevention
Approximate location derived from your IP addressAdvertising, fraud prevention
Interactions with ads (views, taps)Advertising, analytics
Diagnostics (crash and performance data of the ads SDK)Analytics, keeping ads working

Google and its advertising partners (third parties) may place and read cookies, or use device identifiers such as the advertising ID, to show ads, including ads based on your earlier use of this and other apps and websites (personalised ads). Where you do not give consent, or where the law limits it, non-personalised ads are shown; these still use data for frequency limits, measurement and fraud prevention.

This data is sent encrypted. It is processed by Google under its own policies: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Consent and your choices

  • EEA, United Kingdom, Switzerland and other regions where required: before any personalised ads, OffKit All Tools shows Google's consent form (a certified consent management platform). You can change your choice any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • iPhone and iPad: the app asks for permission to track (App Tracking Transparency). If you decline, ads are not personalised using the IDFA.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads).
  • Some US states give you the right to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for targeted advertising; where this applies, Google's consent messages provide that choice.

6. How data is protected

  • Your tool input and files are processed in memory on your device and are not sent anywhere.
  • Settings are stored in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read.
  • All network traffic of the ads SDK uses encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS).

7. What we do not do

  • No accounts or sign-up.
  • OffKit All Tools itself does not collect analytics, contacts, photos, precise location, or anything you type.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

8. Feedback emails

If you send feedback from the app, your email app opens with a message you can edit before sending. If you keep the option on, it includes the app version, operating system version, device model and app language. We use your email only to reply and to improve OffKit All Tools, and delete it when it is no longer needed.

9. Children

OffKit All Tools is a developer tool intended for adults (18 and over). It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information (for example in a feedback email), contact us and we will delete it.

10. Data retention and deletion

We do not hold any of your app data. Data collected by Google for advertising is retained under Google's policies; you can manage it with the choices above. To delete everything stored by OffKit All Tools on your device, uninstall the app. To have a feedback email you sent us deleted, write to the contact address below.

11. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
