Questions and answers

Is Offkit All Tools free?

Yes. The app is free and shows a small banner ad. There is no sign-up and no paid plan.

Is my text uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything you type, paste or open is processed on your phone. It is never sent to us or anyone else. See the privacy policy.

Why does the app need internet?

To show ads (which keep the app free), to check that you have the latest version, and to send anonymous crash reports. Your content is never sent.

Which file types can it open?

Text and JSON files, CSV, Excel .xlsx, Word .docx, Parquet, SVG, PNG, JPEG, WebP and PDF. Files up to 25 MB are supported.

The AI Prompt Builder — does it run AI on my phone?

No. It uses ready-made templates to turn your idea into good prompts. Copy a prompt into the AI app you already use.

How do I change the language?

Settings → Language inside the app. From version 1.2 on Android 13 and newer, you can also pick it in the phone's Settings → Apps → Offkit All Tools → Language.

Can I turn off personalised ads?

Yes. Where the consent form applies (for example in Europe), Settings → Ad privacy choices opens it again. On Android you can also reset or delete your advertising ID in the phone settings.

The app says an update is needed. Why?

Sometimes an older version has a bug that must not stay on phones. Tap Update; it takes a few seconds through Google Play or the App Store.

A tool gave a wrong result or the app crashed.

Please email husnain12.ai42@gmail.com with the tool name and, if possible, a small example. You can also use Settings → Send feedback.

How do I delete my data?

Settings → Clear recent tools removes your history. Uninstalling the app removes everything stored on your phone. For data held by Google (ads, Firebase) see the privacy policy.
